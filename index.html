Hagah

Support

Hagah keeps one Scripture verse with you, on your Home Screen, your Lock Screen, and in your notifications, until it lives in your memory. Then the next arrives.

Common questions

When does the verse change?
At local midnight, at the pace you choose in Settings: daily, every three days, weekly, or every two weeks.

Am I behind on my reading plan?
Never. Plans in Hagah are bookmarks, not calendars. Your next reading waits until you come to it.

Can the reading voice sound better?
Yes. In iOS Settings, open Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices, then English, and download an Enhanced or Premium voice. Hagah uses it automatically.

Where is my data?
On your device and in your personal iCloud. Hagah has no servers and no accounts. See the privacy policy.
