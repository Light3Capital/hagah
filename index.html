Here to help.

Support for Hagah 4.2 on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Email vlad@light3capital.com with your app version, device, and a short description. Please leave private prayers, notes, and backup files out of your message.

How do I change my meditation passage?

In Meditate, open the passage options menu to change your passage. On Mac, use Change passage. In Settings, New passage sets a daily, weekly, or monthly rhythm. You can also keep your current passage longer from its more menu. If it is being kept, Settings offers Resume new passages.

Where are the Biblical and personal prayers?

Open Pray. Biblical prayers contains the Psalms, the Lord's Prayer, prayers of Paul, and other prayers from Scripture. Tap the plus button to add a personal prayer.

How do I find a verse or bring back the reading controls?

In Bible, tap the chapter title. Choose the book and chapter, then a verse, or choose Read chapter. Controls move out of the way while reading; tap the reading area to show them again. Settings includes reading appearance and text size.

Which translations work offline?

The complete King James Version (KJV) is included offline. ESV (2007 text edition) can be downloaded from Downloads. NIV, NKJV and NLT require a connection for uncached passages. Eligible audio chapters, books and recordings can also be downloaded; only provider-approved downloads are offered. Open Downloads in Settings or the Bible menu, or in the Mac sidebar. Translation and recording credits are available in the app.

How do I listen and follow along?

In Bible, choose Listen to play the current chapter. Open the player for playback speed, sleep timer, Read along, and Audio options. Spoken-verse highlighting is available where matching timing exists. You can browse independently and use Back to audio to return, or Listen to this chapter to switch. NIV is text only; recording and testament availability varies by translation.

What if an online passage will not load?

Check your internet connection, use the latest Hagah version, then tap Try again. Online reading uses Apple device verification and is subject to provider availability and service limits. Use KJV to continue reading offline. If the issue persists, contact support with the translation, passage, app version, and error text.

How do I back up or restore my prayers and notes?

Your personal prayers, notes, bookmarks and reading progress are saved on your device and sync through your private iCloud account when available. Open Settings → Your saved data to export or import a backup, or review deleted items. Backups are readable files: save them in a private place. Importing merges the backup with your saved library. Export a backup before removing the app or changing devices.

Are reminders required? What about privacy?

Morning, afternoon, evening and prayer reminders are optional and off until enabled. Hagah has no account requirement, advertising, or tracking. Online Bible requests use a secured service. See our privacy policy for device verification and temporary caching details.
