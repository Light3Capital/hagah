HAGAH · Effective September 19, 2026

Your words are personal.

Hagah is a Bible reading, meditation, and prayer app. No account is required. Hagah has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

Prayers, notes, and saved passages

Hagah saves your prayers, unfinished drafts, notes, bookmarks, and reading-plan progress on your device. Starting with build 44, these also sync through your own private Apple iCloud database when iCloud is available. Personal content is stored in encrypted CloudKit fields. It is not sent to Hagah’s translation service, API.Bible, Bible Brain, or a public database. Local files use Apple’s app sandbox and iOS Data Protection.

Your meditation passage, translation choice, reading place, listening place, and selected preferences also sync between devices using the same iCloud account. Reminder choices and text size stay separate on each device. Offline changes remain saved locally until iCloud is available. Previous saved versions and deleted items are retained for recovery. Your private database uses your iCloud storage; manage Hagah’s iCloud access and stored copies through Apple’s iCloud settings. Apple’s device backups may also include local app data, according to your Apple settings.

Offline and online Bible reading

The complete King James Version is included and can be read and searched without connecting to Hagah’s server. Version 4.2 also offers NIV, ESV, NKJV, NLT, and NASB 1995. The complete ESV 2007 text edition can be downloaded where offered in Downloads. Online translations require a connection when the requested passage is not in an available, unexpired cache.

For online reading, Hagah sends the chosen translation and chapter reference to our service, hosted by Cloudflare. An online keyword search also sends its search phrase. Version 4.2 retrieves NIV from API.Bible and the other online translations from Bible Brain, a service of Faith Comes By Hearing. Older app versions continue to use API.Bible. The requested reference or search phrase reaches the relevant provider, but our service does not forward your app verification identifier or your device’s IP address.

API.Bible passages use an encrypted, bounded on-device cache with an installation-specific key in Apple Keychain, excluded from backups and expiring within 14 days. The service keeps a small shared cache of API.Bible Scripture and search results for up to 14 days. Bible Brain text loaded for online reading stays in a small in-memory cache for no more than one hour. Separately, when you explicitly choose an available translation download, Hagah requests an approved download manifest and stores the permitted text on your device for offline reading and search. These downloaded text files are excluded from backups, do not sync between devices, and can be removed in Downloads. Availability is subject to provider permissions. Search cache keys are hashed, raw search phrases are not persisted by our service, and cached results are not linked to an installation identifier. This is not a personal reading-history database.

Audio and downloads

Listening requests send the selected recording and chapter reference to our service and Bible Brain. The app then connects directly to the provider’s media delivery network to stream the audio or download a chapter where permission is granted. That delivery network receives connection information, including your IP address and the requested media file. Personal prayers and notes are never included in these requests.

Permitted audio downloads are stored in the app sandbox, protected by iOS Data Protection on iPhone and iPad, and excluded from backups. Wi-Fi downloads are the default. Started transfers can continue in the background; open Hagah to continue pending chapters when needed. They do not sync to your other devices. Hagah periodically checks whether a previously approved download is still permitted when you use it online; a temporary connection failure does not remove it. Manage downloaded text and audio in Downloads, available from Settings and the Bible menu, and from the Mac sidebar. Your listening place can sync through your private iCloud account, but audio files are not included in personal-data exports. Apple’s system playback controls can display the chapter, recording, and playback position on the Lock Screen and other supported system surfaces.

Device verification and service protection

Apple’s App Attest verifies that online requests come from a legitimate installation of Hagah. Our service stores the app’s verification key identifier, public verification key, an anti-replay counter, and a last-used time. These records support security and free-service limits. They contain no Apple ID, name, email address, prayer text, or notes, and are removed by daily maintenance after 180 days of inactivity.

Internet connections necessarily disclose connection information, including an IP address, to Cloudflare. Our application uses a keyed hash of that address for short-lived, in-memory rate limiting; it does not write addresses or request bodies to application logs. Rate-limit buckets reset each minute. Cloudflare, Apple, API.Bible, Bible Brain, and the media delivery provider may process service and security information under their own policies. Connections to the translation service and provider use HTTPS.

Your choices and copies

  • Select KJV to read and search without the online translation service.
  • Reminders are optional and scheduled locally. Scripture can appear in notification previews according to your iOS settings. Prayer reminders use generic Biblical wording, never personal prayer text. Widgets show Scripture and reading-plan progress.
  • Sharing or copying a passage sends it only to the destination you choose. Backups exported to Files are readable JSON files containing saved and deleted items and earlier versions. Version 4.2 also includes reading-plan progress and your listening place; older library exports do not. Licensed Bible text and audio files are not included. Choose a private location and protect any copies you share.
  • Deleting an item moves it to Deleted items, including on synced devices, so it can be recovered. Removing Hagah deletes its local app data; exported files, iCloud copies, and Apple device backups are managed separately in Files and your Apple account.

Apple services and support

Apple may collect TestFlight feedback and diagnostics when you use a beta, and processes iCloud, App Attest, and App Store data under its own policies. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid including private prayer or note content unless you want to share it for support.
