Privacy Policy

Hagah collects no data. None.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no servers. The app works entirely offline.

Your favorites, notes, settings, and reading positions are stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through your personal iCloud account, which only you can access. We cannot see any of it.

The app makes no network requests of any kind.
